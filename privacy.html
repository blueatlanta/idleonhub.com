Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This page explains what data is collected when you visit Idleon Hub, who collects it, and what your rights are. We've kept this short and in plain English on purpose.

Short version: Idleon Hub itself doesn't collect or store any data about you. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs basic technical request information for security and performance purposes, as is standard for almost every website on the internet.

// What Idleon Hub collects

Nothing. This site is a static HTML page. It has no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no forms, no accounts, and no advertising scripts. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data about you directly.

// What our hosting provider collects

Idleon Hub is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with virtually all web hosting providers, Cloudflare automatically processes the following request metadata for every visit, for the purposes of security (DDoS protection, bot filtering), performance (caching), and operational reliability:

  • Your IP address
  • Your user agent (browser and operating system)
  • The referring URL (the site you came from, if any)
  • The page you requested and the timestamp of the request
  • Approximate location (country / region) derived from your IP address
  • Connection metadata such as TLS fingerprint

This processing happens at Cloudflare's network edge before any request reaches our site. We do not have direct access to these raw logs and we do not use this data for analytics, marketing, or profiling. For full details on what Cloudflare collects and how it's used, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

// Cookies and local storage

Idleon Hub does not set any cookies and does not use local storage. Cloudflare may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies (for example, to identify legitimate visitors and block malicious traffic). You can review these in Cloudflare's Cookie Policy.

// External links

Idleon Hub is a directory of links to third-party websites (tools, wikis, guides, community pages, official game pages, etc.). Once you click through to any of those sites, their own privacy policies apply, not ours. We have no control over what data those sites collect.

// Donations

If you choose to support the site via the "Buy me a coffee" link, you will be taken to Ko-fi, which is operated independently. Any data collected during the donation process is handled by Ko-fi under their privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

// Email suggestions

If you email a suggestion to the address listed on the site, we'll receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to read and respond to the suggestion. We don't add you to any mailing list and we don't share your email with anyone.

// Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with similar data protection laws, you have rights regarding personal data processed about you — including access, correction, deletion, and objection. Because Idleon Hub itself doesn't collect or store personal data, most requests of this kind should be directed to Cloudflare (for hosting logs) using the contact information in their privacy policy linked above.

If you have any other privacy question relating to this site, you can email us via the contact link on the main page.

// Changes to this policy

If we ever add analytics, advertising, or any other data-collecting feature to the site, we'll update this page first and bump the "Last updated" date at the top. We won't quietly change how data is handled.
